📖 About Chinese Zodiac

The Chinese zodiac, known as Shengxiao (生肖), is a 12-year cycle where each year is represented by an animal sign. Unlike Western astrology which is based on months, the Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar and is deeply rooted in Chinese culture and traditions.

The 12 zodiac animals in order are: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, and Pig. Each sign is also associated with one of the Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water), creating a 60-year cycle.

Note: Chinese zodiac years follow the lunar calendar, so the year changes on Chinese New Year (usually between January 21 and February 20). Our calculator accounts for the exact lunar new year date.
