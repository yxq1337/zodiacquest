Lucky Numbers & Colors by Chinese Zodiac Sign (Complete Guide)

November 10, 2026 · 7 min read

Everyone wants more luck. In Chinese culture, lucky numbers and colors aren't just superstitions — they're practical tools. People use them for everything from choosing phone numbers to picking wedding dates.

Why Numbers Matter in Chinese Culture

Chinese numerology is based on homophones — words that sound like other words. A number is "lucky" if its pronunciation sounds like a word with a positive meaning. It's "unlucky" if it sounds like something bad.

The most famous example: number 4 sounds like "death" (死 sǐ) in Chinese. That's why many Chinese buildings skip the 4th floor.

A Quick Note on "Unlucky"

Don't panic if your unlucky number comes up! "Unlucky" in Chinese metaphysics doesn't mean disaster. It just means the energy is more challenging. Awareness gives you the power to be more careful on those days.

Plus, luck is cumulative. Your good deeds, positive attitude, and hard work create far more good fortune than any number or color ever could.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are my lucky numbers based on my zodiac sign?

Each zodiac sign has specific lucky numbers based on traditional Chinese numerology and the element associated with the sign. Use our tool above to find your lucky numbers, colors, and even lucky days by selecting your zodiac animal.

Why is 8 considered a lucky number in Chinese culture?

The number 8 is considered the luckiest number in Chinese culture because its pronunciation (ba) sounds similar to the word for wealth and prosperity (fa). Many people seek 8 in phone numbers, addresses, and important dates.

What color is lucky for each zodiac sign?

Lucky colors are determined by the element of each zodiac sign: Wood signs (green, brown), Fire signs (red, orange, purple), Earth signs (yellow, brown), Metal signs (white, gold, silver), and Water signs (black, blue, dark gray). Wearing your lucky color is believed to attract good fortune.

Do lucky numbers actually work for lottery?

Lucky numbers based on zodiac signs are part of cultural tradition and personal belief — they don't mathematically increase your odds of winning the lottery. However, many people enjoy using their lucky numbers as a fun way to choose lottery numbers or make decisions.

What are the unlucky numbers in Chinese culture?

The number 4 is the most unlucky in Chinese culture because its pronunciation sounds like the word for "death." The number 7 can also be associated with sadness and ghost month. Many Chinese buildings skip floors numbered 4, similar to how Western buildings skip 13.
