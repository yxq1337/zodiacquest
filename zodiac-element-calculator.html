Understanding the Five Elements

Wu Xing (五行), or the Five Elements, is a fundamental concept in Chinese philosophy. Unlike the Western four elements, these are not physical substances but dynamic energies or phases that describe how things interact and transform.

🌳 Wood (木)

Years ending in: 4 or 5

Growth, creativity, flexibility, leadership, expansion

Season: Spring | Direction: East | Color: Green

🔥 Fire (火)

Years ending in: 6 or 7

Passion, energy, charisma, transformation, warmth

Season: Summer | Direction: South | Color: Red

🪨 Earth (土)

Years ending in: 8 or 9

Stability, reliability, practicality, nurturing, grounding

Season: Late Summer | Direction: Center | Color: Yellow/Brown

⚙️ Metal (金)

Years ending in: 0 or 1

Precision, strength, integrity, justice, refinement

Season: Autumn | Direction: West | Color: White/Gold

💧 Water (水)

Years ending in: 2 or 3

Wisdom, adaptability, intuition, flow, communication

Season: Winter | Direction: North | Color: Blue/Black

The Two Cycles

Generating Cycle (相生): Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood. Each element feeds the next, like a parent nourishing a child.

Controlling Cycle (相克): Wood → Earth → Water → Fire → Metal → Wood. Each element keeps the next in check, maintaining balance.
