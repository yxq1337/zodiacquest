About Chinese Zodiac Lucky Numbers

Lucky numbers in Chinese culture have deep roots in linguistics and numerology. Numbers that sound similar to words with positive meanings are considered lucky, while those resembling words for death or misfortune are avoided.

The Most Lucky Numbers in Chinese Culture

  • 8 (Ba): Sounds like "prosperity" (发 fa). The luckiest number of all.
  • 9 (Jiu): Sounds like "long-lasting" (久 jiu). Associated with eternity and the Emperor.
  • 6 (Liu): Sounds like "smooth" (顺 liu/shun). Brings smooth progress.
  • 2 (Er): Represents harmony and pairs. Good for relationships.

The Most Unlucky Numbers

  • 4 (Si): Sounds almost exactly like "death" (死 si). Universally avoided.
  • 5 (Wu): Sounds like "not" (无 wu). Can negate the positive meaning of other numbers.
  • 7 (Qi): Sounds like "deceive" (欺 qi) and is associated with the ghost month.
