📚 About Zodiac Compatibility

Chinese zodiac compatibility is based on the ancient San He (三合, Three Harmonies) and Liu Chong (六冲, Six Clashes) systems. Each zodiac animal has signs that naturally harmonize with it, and others that may create friction.

The Three Harmonies (San He) are groups of three signs that form the best partnerships: Rat-Dragon-Monkey, Ox-Snake-Rooster, Tiger-Horse-Dog, and Rabbit-Goat-Pig.

The Six Clashes (Liu Chong) are pairs of signs that may find it harder to get along: Rat-Horse, Ox-Goat, Tiger-Monkey, Rabbit-Rooster, Dragon-Dog, and Snake-Pig.
